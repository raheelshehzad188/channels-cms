Returns & Refunds

Changed your mind? You have 30 days from delivery to send anything back.

The short version

  • 30 days from delivery to start a return, for any reason.
  • Items should be unused and in their original packaging where possible.
  • Refunds are issued to the original payment method within five working days of arrival.
  • Faulty or damaged items are refunded in full, including the return postage.

How to send something back

Message us through the contact form with your order number and what you'd like to return. We'll email a prepaid label and a short set of instructions. Drop the parcel at any post office or arrange a collection.

Exceptions

For hygiene reasons we can't accept returns on opened cosmetics, pierced jewellery or unsealed personal care items unless they are faulty. Personalised products are also non-returnable.

Exchanges

We don't run direct exchanges — it's faster to refund the original item and place a new order, so you're not waiting on stock.
